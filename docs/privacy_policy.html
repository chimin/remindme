Privacy Policy for Remind Me

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Introduction

Remind Me ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application ("App") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use Remind Me.

Information We Collect

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we access basic profile information (email address, name, and profile photo, if available) through Google Sign-In to authenticate you and display your account in the App.
  • Google Tasks Data: With your explicit permission, the App accesses and manages your Google Tasks (task lists, tasks, due dates, and task details) via the Google Tasks API.
  • Local Data: The App stores preferences, cached tasks, pending task changes, and home-screen widget data locally on your device using SharedPreferences and Hive. Task changes are synchronized with Google Tasks when a connection and authorization are available.

How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate you with your Google account
  • Read, create, update, and delete tasks in your Google Tasks account as requested by you
  • Provide widget functionality on your device
  • Maintain app preferences

Data Storage and Security

We use the following mechanisms to protect Google user data, including account information, task content, and authentication information:

  • Encryption in Transit: Connections to Google Sign-In and the Google Tasks API use HTTPS/TLS to encrypt Google user data and authentication information during transmission.
  • Authentication and Authorization: We use Google Sign-In and OAuth authorization rather than collecting or storing your Google account password. Access to Google Tasks requires your permission for the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks scope, which allows the App to read, create, update, and delete your task lists and tasks.
  • Local Storage Protection: App preferences, cached tasks, pending changes, and widget data are stored in the App's local storage, using the operating system's app sandbox and access controls. The App does not apply an additional encryption layer to its local Hive cache or SharedPreferences. Task information displayed in a home-screen widget is visible to anyone who can view that screen.
  • Google Tasks Storage: Synchronized tasks are stored in your Google account on Google's servers. The App also keeps a local cache to support offline use. We do not operate a separate server that stores your Google user data.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • Google Tasks: Tasks synchronized to your Google account remain there until you delete them. You can delete tasks through the App or Google Tasks. Deletions made while offline require a successful synchronization before they take effect in Google Tasks.
  • Local Data: Cached tasks, pending changes, and preferences are retained on your device for app functionality until removed by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. Clearing local storage before synchronization discards any unsynchronized changes. Removing local data does not delete tasks already stored in your Google account.
  • Sign Out and Revoke Access: Signing out disconnects the App from Google and clears the home-screen widget data, but does not erase the local task cache or your tasks in Google Tasks. You can also revoke authorization through your Google Account permissions. Revoking access prevents further authorized access to Google Tasks; clear the App's storage separately to remove its local data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. Data is only shared with Google as necessary for Google Tasks synchronization, per Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Sign-In
  • Google Tasks API

Your Rights and Choices

  • Revoke Access: Revoke access via Google Account Permissions.
  • Delete Data: Delete tasks in Google Tasks; clear app data or uninstall to remove local data.
  • Sign Out: Sign out from within the App to disconnect Google access. See Data Retention and Deletion above for how to remove local data.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
