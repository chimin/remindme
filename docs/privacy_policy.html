Privacy Policy for Remind Me

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Introduction

Remind Me ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application ("App") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use Remind Me.

Information We Collect

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we access basic profile information (email address, name) through Google Sign-In for authentication purposes.
  • Google Tasks Data: With your explicit permission, the App accesses and manages your Google Tasks (task lists, tasks, due dates, and task details) via the Google Tasks API.
  • Local Data: The App stores some data locally on your device using SharedPreferences and Hive for app functionality. This data remains on your device.

How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate you with your Google account
  • Read, create, update, and delete tasks in your Google Tasks account as requested by you
  • Provide widget functionality on your device
  • Maintain app preferences

Data Storage and Security

  • Google Tasks: All task data is stored in your Google account via Google's servers. We do not store your tasks on our own servers.
  • Local Storage: App preferences and cached data are stored locally on your device.
  • Authentication: We use Google Sign-In and only request the minimum required scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks).

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. Data is only shared with Google as necessary for Google Tasks synchronization, per Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Sign-In
  • Google Tasks API

Your Rights and Choices

  • Revoke Access: Revoke access via Google Account Permissions.
  • Delete Data: Delete tasks in Google Tasks; clear app data or uninstall to remove local data.
  • Sign Out: Sign out from within the app.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
