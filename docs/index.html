Remind Me

Your day, organized.

Keep everyday tasks in one clear view. Remind Me is a simple task management app that connects to Google Tasks, helping you keep track of what needs doing and check it off when you're done.

Stay on top of tasks

Create and edit tasks, set due dates, and see your to-dos grouped by date. Mark tasks complete as you go.

Sync with Google Tasks

Sign in with your Google account to access your task lists and keep your changes synced with Google Tasks.

A glance from your home screen

Use the Android home screen widget to keep your tasks close at hand, with colors you can customize.

Getting started

Open Remind Me, sign in with Google, and grant access to Google Tasks. Choose a task list and add your first task.
