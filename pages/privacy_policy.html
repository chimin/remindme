Privacy Policy for Remind Me

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Introduction

Remind Me ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application ("App") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use Remind Me.

Information We Collect

Remind Me is designed to sync with Google Tasks. We collect the following types of information:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we access basic profile information (email address, name) through Google Sign-In for authentication purposes.
  • Google Tasks Data: With your explicit permission, the App accesses and manages your Google Tasks (task lists, tasks, due dates, and task details) via the Google Tasks API.
  • Local Data: The App stores some data locally on your device using SharedPreferences and Hive for app functionality (such as widget preferences and cached task data). This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate you with your Google account
  • Read, create, update, and delete tasks in your Google Tasks account as requested by you
  • Provide widget functionality on your device
  • Maintain app preferences and improve app performance

Data Storage and Security

  • Google Tasks: All task data is stored in your Google account via Google's servers. We do not store your tasks on our own servers.
  • Local Storage: App preferences and cached data are stored locally on your device and are not shared with us.
  • Authentication: We use Google Sign-In for secure authentication. We only request the minimum required scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks) needed to access your Google Tasks.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. Your data is only shared with:

  • Google: As necessary to provide Google Tasks synchronization functionality, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In
  • Google Tasks API

Your Rights and Choices

  • Revoke Access: You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time through your Google Account Permissions page.
  • Delete Data: Since tasks are stored in your Google account, you can delete them directly through Google Tasks. Local app data can be cleared by clearing the app data or uninstalling the App.
  • Sign Out: You can sign out of the App at any time through the app settings.

Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
